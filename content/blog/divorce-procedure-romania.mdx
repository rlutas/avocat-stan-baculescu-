## Types of Divorce in Romania

In Romania, there are several ways to obtain a divorce:

### 1. Divorce by Mutual Agreement

When both spouses agree to the divorce and there are no minor children, the procedure can be considerably simplified. The divorce can be pronounced:

- At a notary - the fastest procedure (30 days)
- At the civil status office - if there are no minor children
- In court - with the consent of both spouses

### 2. Judicial Divorce

When spouses cannot reach an agreement, one of them can request divorce in court. Reasons may include:

- Existence of serious grounds that make continuation of the marriage impossible
- De facto separation for at least 2 years
- Health condition of one of the spouses

## Required Documents

To initiate the divorce procedure, you will need:

- Marriage certificate (original)
- Identity documents of both spouses
- Birth certificates of children (if any)
- Proof of residence
- Inventory of common assets (for division)

## Important Aspects

### Child Custody

If there are minor children, the court will decide on:

- Exercise of parental authority
- Establishing children's residence
- Visitation schedule for the non-resident parent
- Child support

### Asset Division

Assets acquired during marriage are considered common property and will be divided equitably.
