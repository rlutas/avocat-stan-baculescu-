## Types of Dismissal

According to the Labor Code, there are several situations in which the employer can terminate the employment contract:

### Dismissal for Disciplinary Reasons

This can occur when the employee:
- Commits a serious misconduct
- Repeatedly violates work obligations
- Has repeated unexcused absences

### Dismissal for Reasons Unrelated to the Employee

The employer can order dismissal in case of:
- Position elimination for economic reasons
- Activity reorganization
- Reduction in the number of employees

## Your Rights

In case of dismissal, you have the right to:

1. **Notice Period** - Minimum 20 working days (or compensation)
2. **Reasoned Decision** - The decision must contain specific reasons
3. **Severance Pay** - In certain cases provided by law or contract
4. **Appeal** - You can contest the decision in court

## How to Contest an Abusive Dismissal

### Steps to Follow:

1. **Request the decision in writing** - This is mandatory
2. **Check the deadlines** - You have 45 days to contest
3. **Gather evidence** - Keep all relevant documents
4. **Consult a lawyer** - For case evaluation

### What the Court Can Obtain:

- Cancellation of the dismissal decision
- Reinstatement
- Payment of salary rights during the appeal period
- Moral damages

## Signs of Abusive Dismissal

Watch out for the following signs:

- Lack of specific reasoning
- Non-compliance with legal procedure
- Discrimination (age, sex, religion, etc.)
- Retaliation for exercising rights
