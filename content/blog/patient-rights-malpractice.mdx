## What is Medical Malpractice?

Medical malpractice refers to an error or negligence by a healthcare professional that causes harm to the patient. These situations can include misdiagnosis, surgical errors, incorrect medication prescriptions, or inadequate care.

## Your Rights as a Patient

In Romania, patients have well-defined rights under the law:

1. **Right to Information** - You have the right to be fully informed about your diagnosis and proposed treatment
2. **Right to Informed Consent** - No medical procedure can be performed without your consent
3. **Right to Compensation** - In case of medical error, you have the right to seek compensation

## How to Act in Case of Malpractice?

If you suspect you have been a victim of medical error:

- **Document Everything** - Keep all medical records
- **Seek a Second Opinion** - Another doctor can evaluate the situation
- **Consult a Specialized Lawyer** - A lawyer experienced in malpractice can guide you

## Statute of Limitations

It is important to know that there is a statute of limitations for legal actions. Generally, you have 3 years from the date you learned about the harm to take action.
